OFFSCREEN

Privacy Policy

Effective 7 October 2026 · Last updated 7 October 2026

OFFSCREEN collects nothing about you. The game has no ability to send anything anywhere, so nothing leaves your phone.

Why that claim is verifiable

OFFSCREEN is a single-player game for Android. It requests no Android permissions at all, including INTERNET. Without that permission the operating system itself blocks the app from opening any network connection. There is no account, no server, no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting in the build. The game works identically in aeroplane mode.

What the game stores, and where

The game saves your progress so you can carry on where you left off:

  • which levels you have unlocked and finished
  • your best time and death count for each level
  • which secrets and badges you have found
  • your settings, such as volume, vibration and the accessibility options
  • total play time, deaths and attempts

All of it is written to the app's own private storage on your phone. Android prevents other apps from reading it, and the developer never receives it.

Android backup

If you have turned on Android's own device backup, Android may include this save data in your phone's backup to your Google account, so your progress survives a phone change. That backup is made by Android under your Google account and Google's privacy policy. The game itself uploads nothing, and the developer has no access to it.

Deleting your data

  • In the game, open Settings and choose Erase progress. This deletes your saved progress and keeps your settings.
  • Uninstall the app. Android deletes everything the app stored.

There is nothing to request from us, because nothing is held by us.

Children

The game contains no advertising, no purchases, no chat, no user-generated content and no data collection of any kind. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either.

Third parties

The game is built with Flutter and uses two open-source packages: shared_preferences, which reads and writes the save file described above, and audioplayers, which plays the game's sound. Neither collects, transmits or shares any information. No data is sold, shared or disclosed, because none is gathered.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of OFFSCREEN ever handles data differently, this page will be updated before that version ships, and the date at the top will change.
